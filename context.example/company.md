# Company: Acme Radiography (fictional)

Copy this folder to `context/` and replace every file with your own company's
information. `context/` is gitignored.

## What we sell

- **Digital dental x-ray.** Intraoral sensors, panoramic units, and install,
  training, and service for dental practices.
- **Inline x-ray inspection.** Foreign-object detection units that mount over
  an existing production conveyor, for food, pharmaceutical, and packaging lines.
  Sold direct and through conveyor manufacturers who spec them into new lines.

## How we sell

- Dental: direct, one practice at a time. Install within two weeks of order.
- Inspection: direct to plants, and as a component partner to conveyor
  manufacturers, who add our unit to their quotes.

## Where we operate

Service technicians cover the Pacific Northwest and the Great Lakes states.
Outside those regions we sell equipment but cannot offer same-week service.
