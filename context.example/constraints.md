# Constraints (fictional)

Rules for what the judge and every draft must not say.

- Never promise a result not in `proof.md`, or a better number than the one written there.
- Never claim to know a company's current equipment, budget, or problems.
  Public data shows what they are and where; everything past that is a guess and
  must be phrased as one.
- Never name existing customers. Describe them by type ("a food packaging plant").
- No regulatory claims about radiation safety or compliance in outreach. Those
  conversations happen with a qualified specialist.
- No fake urgency and no flattery.
- Drafts stay under 120 words.
