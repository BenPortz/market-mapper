# Ideal customer (fictional)

The judge uses this file to decide fit. Be specific about who does *not* buy;
that is what stops a model from calling every company a fit.

## Dental practices

**Good fit**
- Independent or small-group practices (1 to 5 locations).
- Recently opened, relocated, or expanded. New operatories need imaging.
- General dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery, orthodontics.

**Poor fit**
- Large dental service organizations. Equipment is bought centrally under
  national contracts we are not on.
- Dental schools and public health clinics with procurement cycles over a year.
- Mobile and teledentistry services.

## Conveyor manufacturers

**Good fit (component partner)**
- Build conveyors for food, beverage, pharmaceutical, or packaging lines, where
  inspection is often a line requirement.
- Design and fabricate custom systems, not only resell standard sections.

**Poor fit**
- Bulk material and mining conveyors. No inspection requirement.
- Used equipment dealers and belt repair services.
- Distributors with no engineering team.
