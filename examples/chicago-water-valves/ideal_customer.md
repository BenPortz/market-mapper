# Who belongs on the list

## Chicago water valve manufacturers / Good fit
Companies in the Chicago metro that manufacture (design and build, not just
resell) valves whose main use is water:
- Waterworks and wastewater valves (butterfly, check, air release, plug, gate).
- Plumbing valves: flush valves, faucet and fixture valves, thermostatic and tempering mixing valves, backflow preventers.
- Hydronic heating and chilled water valves (balancing, check, flow control).
- Water check, float, and pressure control valves for building and process water.

## Chicago water valve manufacturers / Weaker fit
- General industrial valve makers that list water among many media (air, oil, gas, steam).
- Companies whose valves are a component of a water product (fountains, eyewash stations).
- Brands owned by a larger group where manufacturing in Chicago is not confirmed.

## Chicago water valve manufacturers / Not on the list
- Distributors, reps, and supply houses.
- Valves mainly for hydraulics, oil and gas, railcars, or other non-water service.
- Sensors and switches that are not valves.
- Manufacturing that has moved out of the Chicago metro.
